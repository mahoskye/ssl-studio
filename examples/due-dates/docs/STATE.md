# Where Due dates stands

As of 2026-11-19. Written to be read aloud to someone who is not an
engineer: no file names, no code.

## Where things stand

Samples that arrive without a due date now get one, counted in working days
and moved past any day their instrument is out of service. The report of
what is due soon is planned and not yet built. Nothing here has run in
STARLIMS itself: it has run in a local sandbox only.

## What works now

- A due date is set for every waiting sample, for routine and for rush work.
- A sample that cannot be given a date is skipped, and the reason is kept.
- Either every date is saved or none is.

## What is half-built

- The report of samples due soon reads the right samples. It does not yet
  mark the ones that are late.

## What is blocked, and on what

- Holidays for more than one site: nobody has said yet which holidays each
  site keeps.
