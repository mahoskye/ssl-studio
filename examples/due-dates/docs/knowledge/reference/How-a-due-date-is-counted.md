# How a due date is counted

The rule in words, for someone who has not read the scripts.

1. Start on the day after the sample was received.
2. Step forward a day at a time. Count a day when it is a working day and
   the sample's instrument is in service.
3. Stop when as many days are counted as the turnaround asks for. That day
   is the due date.

A working day is not a Sunday, not a holiday, and for routine work not a
Saturday. Rush work counts Saturday.

The walk stops after 730 days and gives no date
([decision 1](../decisions/0001-A-walk-looks-at-most-730-days-ahead.md)).
[The worked examples](../../Worked-examples.md) are counted by hand this way.
